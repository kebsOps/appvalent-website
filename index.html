About us

Small team,
big ambitions.

Appvalent Limited is an indie app studio dedicated to building high-quality mobile apps that make a meaningful difference in everyday life.

No investors to answer to and no shortcuts — just a focused team that owns every detail, from the first sketch to the hundredth update.

Quality first

Every pixel, interaction, and feature is considered carefully before it ships.

Privacy minded

We collect only what's necessary and never sell user data to third parties.

Always improving

We listen to our users and ship regular updates based on real feedback.

Built to last

We're in this for the long run — our apps are maintained and supported over time.
