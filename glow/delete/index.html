Delete Your Data or Account

Last updated 15 October 2026

The short version. Your photos and scans are on your phone, and you can delete them yourself in the app right now. To delete the account record we hold, email support@appvalent.com and we will action it within 30 days.

These are two separate things, and you do not have to do the bigger one to get the smaller one. You do not need an account to use Glow: Face Score at all — signing in with Apple or Google is optional — so the second section below only applies if you signed in.

1. Delete some of your data, and keep your account

  • Your photos and scans. In the app, open a result and delete that scan, or delete all of them at once from Profile. Both remove the photo from the phone, not just the score. Uninstalling the app removes every one of them.
  • Your reminder and device record. Turn the notifications master toggle off in Profile, or turn notifications off in your system settings. We delete your device record — push token, platform, time zone, reminder time and reminder frequency — as soon as you do.
  • Anything else. Email support@appvalent.com with the subject Delete my data, telling us what you want removed. We action it within 30 days and confirm by email. Your account stays open.

2. Delete your whole account

  • Email support@appvalent.com from the address you signed in with, using the subject Delete my account.
  • If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your email, send us the private relay address Apple gave us, or tell us the name on the account so we can find it.
  • We confirm by email once it is done. We action every request within 30 days of receiving it.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store and Google Play, and we never receive your payment details. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — cancel that in the App Store or in Google Play, otherwise it keeps renewing.
