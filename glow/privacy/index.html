Glow: Face Score

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

The short version. Your photos never leave your phone. They are analysed on the device and saved in the app's own private storage. We never upload them, never see them, and never share them.

What the app does with your photos

When you take a scan, the photo is captured by the camera, analysed on your device, and saved to the app's private storage on that device. The analysis is a set of image measurements — brightness, sharpness, local contrast, colour variance and the proportion of bright, desaturated pixels. No photo, and no data derived from a photo, is sent to us or to anyone else.

The app uses your operating system's face detector (Apple's Vision framework on iOS, ML Kit on Android) to locate the face in the frame so the measurements run on your face rather than the background. That detector runs on the device. It returns a rectangle. It does not build, store or transmit a faceprint, and the app does not attempt to identify who you are from a photo.

Photos stay until you delete the scan or uninstall the app. Delete a single scan from its result screen, or all of them at once from Profile — both remove the photo from the phone, not just the score. If you tap Share on a result, your operating system's share sheet opens and you choose where the image goes — that is your action, to a destination you pick, and we are not involved in it.

Your photos are also left out of your phone's cloud backup. iCloud Backup and Android Auto Backup would otherwise copy them to your Apple or Google account along with everything else on the phone. We exclude them, because "stays on your device" should mean that. The trade is real and worth knowing: your scores and history do back up and will restore on a new phone, but the photos will not — those cards will say the photo is unavailable.

What we do collect

That is the complete list. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting service and no third-party trackers.

If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive only Apple's private relay address. We never receive your real one.

Where that data is held

On Cloudflare's network, in a database we control, reachable only by the app and by us. It is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers. We share it only where we are legally required to, or with the infrastructure providers named below who process it on our behalf.

Who else is involved

  • Apple and Google — sign-in, push delivery, and all purchases. Subscriptions are handled entirely by the App Store and Google Play; we never see your card or payment details.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the reminder service and its database.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivers push notifications on Android.

Your choices

  • Notifications. Turn them off in Profile, or in your system settings. When you turn the master toggle off, we delete your device record.
  • Your account. Sign out in Profile at any time. Email support@appvalent.com to have your account and everything attached to it deleted; we will action it within 30 days.
  • Your photos and scans. They are on your device. Delete a scan in the app, or uninstall to remove all of them.

Children

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or how we use it, we will update the date at the top and describe the change in the app's release notes.
