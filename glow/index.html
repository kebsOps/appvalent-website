Features

Six measurements,
tracked over time

A score from your camera

Take a selfie and Glow measures it — brightness, sharpness, colour evenness and how much light your skin reflects — then turns those into one number.

Trends, not verdicts

One score means little on its own. Glow keeps your history so you can see the direction it moves across weeks, which is the part that actually tells you something.

It tells you when the light changed

Lighting moves the score. If a scan was taken in a brighter or dimmer room than the last one, Glow says so instead of letting you read it as a change in your skin.

A routine built from your scans

Morning and night steps chosen from your own lowest scores and the concerns you picked — product categories, never brands, and never a paid placement.

Reminders on your schedule

Pick the time and how often — every day, three times a week, or weekly. The reminder arrives in your own time zone, or not at all if you'd rather.

Your photos stay on your phone

Every scan is analysed on the device and saved in the app's own storage. We never upload your photos, never see them, and have no way to.

"A tracker, not a verdict."

Glow measures your photo, not your skin. There is no trained skin model and no dermatologist behind the number — it is a set of image measurements, computed on your device, that move when your photo moves. That makes it useful for watching change in similar conditions, and a poor way to judge yourself against anyone else. It is not a medical device and cannot detect any condition.
