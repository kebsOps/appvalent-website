Terms of Service

Last updated 25 September 2026

What this app is. Glow: Face Score takes a photo on your phone and measures it — how bright it is, how sharp, how evenly coloured, how much it reflects light. Those measurements become a score you can watch over time. It is a self-care tracking tool, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose anything.

What the score is, and isn't

The score is computed from the pixels of your photo by a fixed set of image measurements. It is not produced by a trained skin-analysis model, and no dermatologist has reviewed it.

Because it measures the photo, it is affected by the conditions the photo was taken in. Lighting, camera angle, distance and whether you are wearing makeup all move the number. Two photos taken minutes apart in different rooms can score differently. The app tells you when a scan was taken in materially different light from the one before it. The score is most useful when you scan in similar conditions each time and watch the direction it moves, and least useful when read as an absolute measurement of your skin.

Nothing in this app is medical advice. It cannot detect skin cancer, infection, allergy or any other condition, and an encouraging score is not evidence that nothing is wrong. If you have a concern about your skin, see a doctor or a dermatologist. Do not use this app to decide whether to seek care.

Any product suggestion the app makes is a product category — a moisturiser, a cleanser, an SPF. We do not recommend brands, we are not paid to suggest anything, and we have no commercial relationship with any skincare company. Patch-test new products and follow their instructions.
