Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

The short version. Your heartbeat recordings, journal entries and everything you type stay on your phone. We don't run a server that stores them, and we never receive them. The one thing that does leave your device is advertising data, and only if you're not subscribed.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Blumly is written to your phone's own storage and nowhere else:

  • Heartbeat recordings made with your microphone
  • Journal entries, notes and how you're feeling
  • Kick counts and session history
  • Your name, due date and current pregnancy week
  • Your conversations with Nova

We do not operate a server that holds any of it. Deleting an entry removes it, and uninstalling the app removes all of it. If you want a recording somewhere else, you share it yourself, to a destination you choose.

About Nova

Nova's replies are written into the app and chosen on your device based on what you type and how far along you are. Nothing you say to Nova is sent anywhere — there is no server, no AI provider, and no request leaves your phone. Your conversation is stored locally along with the rest of your data.

Nova is a companion, not a clinician. It cannot assess a symptom and it is not a substitute for your midwife or doctor.

Advertising

If you are not subscribed, Blumly shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob receives your device's advertising ID and standard ad-request information — it does not receive your recordings, your journal, or anything you have typed into the app.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation entirely, in your Android settings under Settings → Google → Ads. Subscribing also removes ads. Google's handling of that data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your card or payment details. Google tells the app whether a subscription is active, and nothing more.

Children

Blumly is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete your data. It is on your phone — delete individual entries in the app, or uninstall to remove everything.
  • Ads. Reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings, or subscribe to remove ads.
  • Questions. Email support@appvalent.com.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or how we use it, we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes.
